How to play

One pack at a time.

  1. Swipe the shelf Move between packs with a swipe or the arrows. Each one shows its price, range, and average.
  2. Check the odds View Odds lists every rarity and the virtual value it can pay inside that pack.
  3. Rip it open Drag across the wax. The card rises, flips, and the rarer it is, the louder the reveal.
  4. Sell or keep Selling adds the card’s value to your bank. Keeping it goes into the collection, which stays saved.

The bank returns to $100 every refresh. The binder does not. If you cannot afford a pack and have nothing left to sell, you bust. There is no way to add real money.

FAQ

Before you rip.

Is this real money?

No. The bank, pack prices, and card values are play money inside the game. Nothing is charged and nothing is paid out.

What happens if I refresh?

Your bank goes back to $100. Cards you kept, pull history, and the limited-pack supply stay on this device.

What is the $100,000 pack?

An extremely limited pack with its own look and exclusive cards. Only 10 exist. When they are gone, it says SOLD OUT.

Where do I play?

Open the game from this site. It runs in the browser on your computer. Your collection is saved in this browser.
